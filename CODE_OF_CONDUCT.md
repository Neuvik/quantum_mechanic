# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include using
welcoming language, respecting differing viewpoints, accepting constructive
feedback, and focusing on what is best for the community. Harassment, insults,
threats, and publication of others' private information without permission are
not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to security@neuvik.com. Maintainers may remove,
edit, or reject contributions and take other appropriate action in response to
conduct they determine to be inappropriate or harmful.

This policy is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
